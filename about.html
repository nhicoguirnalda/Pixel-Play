About PixelPlay

We are a small, independent team on a simple mission: make genuinely fun browser games that anyone can play in seconds — for free, on any device, with no strings attached.

Our story and mission

PixelPlay started with a frustration. The web is full of game portals, but many of them bury a decent game behind install prompts, sign-up walls, endless pop-ups and aggressive advertising. We wanted the opposite: a clean site where you click a thumbnail and you are simply playing, within a couple of seconds. That single idea — remove every obstacle between a player and a game — is still the only rule we follow when we add anything new to the site.

Our mission is to build and publish small, polished HTML5 games that respect your time, your attention and your hardware. We are not trying to be the biggest portal on the web. We would rather be the one you bookmark because it always works.

How PixelPlay works

Every game on PixelPlay runs directly in your web browser using standard HTML5, CSS and JavaScript. There is nothing to download, nothing to install and no plugin to enable — if your browser can load this page, it can play our games. Just open a game, press play, and your score starts counting.

Progress is stored locally, in your own browser. High scores, your recently played list and settings such as sound preferences are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, which is why there is no account system and why your data never leaves your computer or phone. If you clear your browser data, your local scores go with it — that is the trade-off for playing with zero registration.

Our games

Every title on PixelPlay is an original game, written from scratch in-house by our own developers. We do not host or re-upload games from other portals, and we do not licence third-party game libraries. That means our catalogue is deliberately small — currently a handful of carefully made titles — but each one has been tested, tuned and polished until it feels right.

We release new games gradually rather than in bulk, because we would rather ship one solid game than five rushed ones. If you enjoy the classics, try Neon Snake or Pong Arena; if you prefer something more relaxed, Merge 2048 is a good place to start. More original titles are in development.

How the site is funded

PixelPlay is free to use and always will be. Like most free websites, we pay for hosting and development through display advertising: you will see a clearly marked ad banner on some pages. Those ads are what keep every game free for everyone, and we deliberately keep them modest so they never get in the way of play.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not run popup advertising, fake download buttons or misleading "you are the 1,000,000th visitor" tricks. For the full picture, including your advertising choices, read our Privacy Policy.

Devices and performance

We build for real-world hardware, not just flagship machines. The whole site and every game are designed to run smoothly on older laptops, low-end Android phones, tablets and desktop computers alike, and they work over slow connections because we keep the download weight of each game small.

Controls adapt to what you are using: keyboard and mouse on a computer, on-screen buttons and swipe gestures on a touchscreen. Games scale from a phone screen up to a large monitor without losing clarity, and no game requires you to install anything or grant any special permissions.

Frequently asked questions

Is PixelPlay really free?

Yes — every game is free to play, with no premium tier, no in-game purchases and no locked levels. Display ads on the site cover the costs.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration, no login and no email required. You can open the site and start playing immediately.

Is my progress saved?

High scores and your recently played list are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing browser data removes them.

Can I suggest a game?

Absolutely — we read every message. Head to our contact page and tell us what you would like to play next.
