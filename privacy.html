Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026. Last updated: 17 October 2026. This policy explains what information PixelPlay handles, why, and what choices you have — in plain language wherever possible.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

PixelPlay is an independent browser-game portal that publishes free, original HTML5 games. We are not a large corporation and we do not operate a user database: there are no accounts, no profiles and no sign-up on this site.

For any privacy question, request or complaint, email us at hello@yourdomain.com. We answer privacy enquiries ourselves, and we aim to respond within 30 days (sooner in most cases).

2. Information we collect

No registration data. You never have to create an account, give us your name, or hand over an email address to play. If you choose to contact us through the contact page, we receive whatever you type into the form — your name, your email address and your message — used for nothing except replying to you.

Data stored locally in your browser. PixelPlay keeps small pieces of information in your browser's local storage (localStorage) on your own device. This includes your high scores, your recently played games list, and settings such as sound preferences. This data stays on your device: it is not transmitted to our servers when you play, and it cannot be read by other people using a different device or browser.

You are in control of it. Because it lives in your browser, you can erase it at any time through your browser's settings (usually under "Clear browsing data" or "Site settings → Delete data"). Clearing it will also reset your saved scores and preferences on this site.

3. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display advertising on some pages. Advertising is what pays for hosting and keeps every game free, and it is also the main reason cookies are set on this site.

Google and its partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick DART cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. These cookies let ad networks remember that you have visited a site so they can show you ads they think are relevant to you.

You have clear choices:

  • Opt out of personalised advertising served by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies used for advertising across many websites at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Learn how Google uses data when you visit or use our site (and how partners may use cookies) at Google's page on how Google uses data from partner sites.

Please note: we do not control these third-party practices. Google decides which ads are shown and manages its own cookies, and advertising partners may combine information collected from this site with information they collected from other websites or apps you have visited, in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can also block or delete cookies entirely through your browser settings, though some site features (including saved scores) may then stop working.

4. Analytics and measurement

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregated measurement to understand basic usage of the site — which pages are visited, which games are popular, and whether pages load correctly. These statistics are counted in aggregate: they help us decide which games to improve or build next, and we do not use them to build individual profiles or to follow you around the internet.

5. Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on consent for non-essential cookies and advertising (which you can withdraw at any time using the links above), and on our legitimate interest in operating, securing and improving the site for the rest of our processing.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectify inaccurate data;
  • Erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • Restrict how we process it;
  • Object to processing, including profiling;
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Under the California CCPA: know what personal information is collected, delete it, and opt out of its "sale" or sharing for targeted advertising;
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you service or provide a different quality of service because you exercised these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@yourdomain.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and some data (such as records already legally required to be kept) may not be deletable.

6. Children's privacy

PixelPlay is family-friendly, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or under 16 where a higher age applies in the European Union. Because the site requires no registration, children typically provide us with no personal data at all. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at hello@yourdomain.com and we will remove it promptly.

7. Data retention

Contact-form messages are kept only as long as we need them to handle your enquiry, and deleted afterwards. Local storage entries stay on your device until you clear them or we change the storage keys in an update. Advertising and analytics records are retained by their providers for the periods described in their own policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures — HTTPS in transit, minimal data collection, access limited to the people who run the site — to protect what little information we hold. However, no system connected to the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any transmission or storage.

9. International transfers

Our site is served globally, and advertising and analytics providers such as Google may process data in countries outside your own — including the United States. Where such transfers take place, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions, as applicable.

10. Links to third-party sites

PixelPlay may link to external websites, and our pages contain advertisements that lead to third-party destinations. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted here for a reasonable period. We encourage you to check back occasionally.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns or requests about anything on this page? Email hello@yourdomain.com and a human will reply. You can also read our Terms of Use, which cover the rules for using the site, and our About page for how PixelPlay works.
