How to Play Breach Point

Breach Point drops you at the door of a hostile compound: eight connected rooms full of patrolling guard bots, one global clock and one extraction pad on the far side of the map. Sweep every room, then step onto the extraction tile before the timer hits zero. WASD walks and strafes, and the mouse turns your view — click the game screen once to lock the pointer, or steer with the arrow keys if you prefer to keep your hands on the keyboard.

Each guarded room holds two or three bots in three weight classes: nimble pink scouts that rush you, standard red guards that hold angles, and chunky orange heavies that soak up extra shots. Bots patrol until they spot you in their line of sight, then open fire — keep moving, use doorways as funnels, and grab the two medkits stashed in the compound when your health runs low.

Controls

  • WASD to walk and strafe, ← → to turn
  • Click the screen to lock the mouse, then move it to look around the room — Esc pauses and releases it
  • Left click or F to fire; the blaster auto-reloads after eight shots
  • Space to jump — hop over a guard's line of fire as you cross a doorway
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the run
  • On a phone: use the on-screen turn, walk, fire and jump buttons

Tactics

The waypoint arrow and the corner minimap always point at the next room that still has bots, so you never have to guess where to go. Clearing a room pays a bonus, and the counter above the crosshair shows who is left in the room you are standing in. Your shots are instant-hit, so snap to a bot's centre and fire in short bursts — ammunition is unlimited, but the reload pause is not.

Scoring

  • +120 to +220 per bot destroyed, depending on how tough it was
  • +250 for clearing every bot out of a room
  • +25 per medkit, and +300 for reaching the extraction pad
  • Speed bonus: +5 for every second left on the clock at extraction

Accuracy is tracked from your first shot, and your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
