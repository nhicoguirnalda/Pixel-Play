How to Play Strike Zone

Strike Zone is a first-person run through three glowing grid mazes. You see the corridor through your own eyes: walls stretch away with real depth, the corner minimap tracks every step, and hostile training bots patrol the rooms — clumsy robots with a single glowing eye, not living targets. Each zone fills up as you clear it, so the first room is a warm-up and the third expects quick reflexes. Destroy every bot in a room and the blast doors open onto the next, harder maze.

You carry 100 health and a 14-round magazine. Bots notice you on sight or when they hear your blaster, then walk straight toward you and open fire with shaky aim — their eye flashes a beat before each shot, which is your cue to strafe sideways. When the magazine runs dry the blaster reloads itself after a short delay, so duck behind a wall at that moment. Your best score is saved in this browser for the next run.

Controls

  • WASD to walk and strafe through the maze
  • ← → to turn, or click the view once to lock the mouse and look around
  • Space to jump — bounce over a bot's shaky bolts mid-fight
  • F or the left mouse button to fire — the magazine reloads by itself
  • P, Esc or Enter to pause, R to restart the run
  • On a phone: use the ⟲ / ▲ / 🔥 / ▼ / ⟳ buttons under the arena

Scoring and the scoreboard

Every bot you destroy is worth 100 points times the zone number, and clearing a zone pays 250 points times the zone number. The HUD tracks Score, Best, HP and Level live, with your magazine count beside them, so you always know whether to push or fall back and let the reload finish.

Tips for clearing the zones

  • Strafe, don't back up — sideways movement is what actually dodges incoming shots.
  • Watch the minimap: bots light up as dots before they round the corner.
  • Let bots come to you. Holding a doorway means only one robot can shoot at a time.
  • Reload behind cover; an empty magazine at close range is the fastest way to lose HP.
  • Fire a single test shot to sound the alarm — then every bot in the room walks into view.
