Hook Hero

How to Play Hook Hero

Hook Hero is a pendulum-swinging adventure over a bottomless neon chasm. Your stickman hero starts each stage suspended in the void, and the only way across is the glowing anchor rings floating ahead. Hold your hook to shoot a rope to the nearest ring, swing like a pendulum gathering speed, then release at the perfect moment to fly through the air on pure momentum.

Real pendulum physics are at work the whole time: gravity pulls you down, the rope tugs you around its anchor, and the swing keeps a gentle forward pull so good timing is always rewarded with progress. Twelve hand-built stages stretch across the chasm, each ending at a shining finish gate. Later stages add crosswinds that shove your swing sideways and spark sentries that sweep across the flight line, and they shorten your hook reach while strengthening gravity. Clear a stage to unlock the next one, or jump straight to any stage you have already reached with the numbered buttons under the canvas. Falling into the chasm simply restarts that stage — the coins you already collected stay collected.

Controls

  • Hold Space (or hold the mouse button on the game, or the on-screen Hook button) to attach the rope
  • Release Space to let go and fly forward on momentum
  • Pick any unlocked stage from the numbered buttons beneath the canvas
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the run, Space to start

Tips for crossing the chasm

  • Hook the ring ahead of you, not the one you are already passing — the swing throws you forward.
  • Let the swing build. Two or three pumps on a ring carry far further than one desperate release.
  • Watch the wind streaks: a gust pushing right means you should release a little later than usual.
  • Spark sentries patrol a fixed lane. Pass above or below one, or wait on the rope until it swings clear.
  • Snatch the floating coins along your flight line; they are worth 25 points each.

Scoring

Every stage you clear is worth 250 points, plus a speed bonus for beating the stage's par time. Coins add 25 points each the moment you touch them, and the HUD keeps Score, Best, Level and Coins live while you swing. Clear all twelve stages in one run to win the game.
