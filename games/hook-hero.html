Hook Hero

How to Play Hook Hero

Hook Hero is a pendulum-swinging adventure over a bottomless neon chasm. Your stickman hero starts each stage suspended in the void, and the only way across is the glowing anchor rings floating ahead. Hold your hook to shoot a rope to the nearest ring, swing like a pendulum gathering speed, then release at the perfect moment to fly through the air on pure momentum. Land another hook on the next ring before you drop into the dark.

Real pendulum physics are at work the whole time: gravity pulls you down, the rope tugs you around its anchor, and the swing keeps a gentle forward pull so good timing is always rewarded with progress. Six hand-built stages stretch across the chasm, each ending at a shining finish gate. Miss a hook and fall below the screen and you simply restart that stage — you keep your score, and the coins you already collected stay collected, so every attempt carries you a little further.

Controls

  • Hold Space (or hold the mouse button on the game, or the on-screen Hook button) to attach the rope
  • Release Space to let go and fly forward on momentum
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the run, Space to start

Tips for crossing the chasm

  • Hook the ring ahead of you, not the one you are already passing — the swing throws you forward.
  • Let the swing build. Two or three pumps on a ring carry far further than one desperate release.
  • Snatch the floating coins along your flight line; they are worth 25 points each.
  • Finishing a stage quickly pays a speed bonus on top of the 250-point level bonus.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.

Scoring

Every stage you clear is worth 250 points, plus a speed bonus for beating the stage's par time. Coins add 25 points each the moment you touch them, and the HUD keeps Score, Best, Level and Coins live while you swing. Clear all six stages in one run to win the game — and post the biggest score your browser has ever seen for Hook Hero.
