Word Hunt

How to Play Word Hunt

Every round of Word Hunt hides ten everyday words inside a fresh 10×10 neon letter grid. The words run forwards and backwards, across rows, down columns and along both diagonals, so a word can begin at any edge and travel in almost any direction. Nothing is listed for you in advance — the ten words stay secret until you trace them, and each one you uncover lights up along the bottom of the board as a coloured pill.

To grab a word, press down on its first letter and drag in a straight line to its last letter, then release. The trail glows behind your finger or cursor as you drag. If the letters spell one of the hidden words, the path locks into colour and joins your found list; any other line flashes red and clears, so a wild swipe costs nothing but a moment. On a touchscreen you can simply tap the start cell and then tap the end cell instead of dragging.

You begin each puzzle with 120 seconds on the clock, and every word you find adds three seconds back. A word scores its length multiplied by ten, plus a bonus for longer finds. Clear all ten words and the board celebrates, pays out 200 points plus five points for every remaining second, then hands you a brand-new grid with extra time on the clock. When the timer finally reaches zero, the results card shows your score, your best score, how many words you uncovered overall and your longest find. Your best score is saved in this browser so there is always a target to beat.

Controls

  • Press and drag from the first letter to the last letter to select a word
  • On a phone or tablet: tap the start cell, then tap the end cell
  • Space starts a game, P or Esc pauses, R restarts

Tips for a high score

  • Work the diagonals first — they are the hardest lines to spot by eye.
  • Long words pay double, so hunt the seven- and eight-letter chains early.
  • Found words tint their cells; use the tinted letters as anchors for crossing words.
  • Clear grids fast — the seconds-left bonus is where the really big scores hide.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
