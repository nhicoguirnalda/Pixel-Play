How to Play Sky Archer

Sky Archer puts you on a cliff overlooking a valley of floating towers, each one carrying a target — painted training dummies, clockwork robots and stick-figure silhouettes standing at different distances. Your crosshair follows the mouse anywhere over the range. Hold the mouse button (or Space) to draw the bow: the power bar fills in about a second, and the longer you hold, the truer the shot. A quick tap looses a wild arrow, so patient draws are what hit the gold.

Arrows fall as they fly, and a crosswind pushes every shot sideways — the vane in the top corner shows the direction and the number to read. Distant towers pay more, so you must lead a far target against both gravity and wind. Each round gives you eight arrows to reach a points quota: clear it and the wind changes as you move further back; miss it and the day is over. Three rounds stand between you and victory, and your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • Move the mouse to place the crosshair; hold the left button to draw and release to loose
  • Space draws and releases too — use ↑↓←→ or WASD to aim without a mouse
  • P, Esc or Enter to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: hold the range to draw and release to loose, or nudge the aim with the on-screen buttons

Tips for reading the wind

  • Full draw is precise; anything under half power adds a noticeable wobble to the arrow.
  • Watch the vane before you draw. Aim slightly into the wind and the drift carries you back onto the target.
  • Gravity bites on far shots — aim a little above a distant tower and let the arrow drop.
  • Back-to-back bullseyes build a streak bonus worth 25 points more per gold, so accuracy beats rushing.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.

Scoring

The gold centre of a target scores 100, the body ring 50 and the outer ring 25 — each multiplied by how far out the tower sits (×1, ×1.5 or ×2). The HUD keeps Score, Best, Round and Arrows live, and the range shows your quota progress. Clear all three rounds to win the contest.
