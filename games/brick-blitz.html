How to Play Brick Blitz

Brick Blitz is a fast game of angles. You control a glowing paddle at the bottom of the 640×480 playfield, and one very bouncy ball does the rest. Five rows of bricks hang near the top — the highest rows pay the most, so pick off the red and orange bricks early while the ball still travels slowly. Every cleared wall earns a 100 point bonus, rebuilds the bricks and speeds the ball up by about six percent. You get three lives per run, and where the ball touches your paddle decides where it goes next: hit it dead centre and it bounces straight back up, clip it on the edge and it leaves at a steep angle. Learning to steer the ball with tiny paddle nudges is the whole trick.

Keep the paddle moving — one sitting still is usually one watching the ball sail past. After each lost life the ball sticks to your paddle again, so take a breath, aim your first shot away from the wall and send the ball digging through the middle of the stack. Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.

Controls

  • Move the mouse over the board, or drag a finger across it, to guide the paddle
  • ← → or AD to nudge the paddle at a steady speed
  • Space or Enter to start and launch, P to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the on-screen pad — ◀▶ steer, ▲ launches the ball, ▼ pauses

Tips for a high score

  • Aim at the top two rows first — 50 and 40 points each while the ball is at its slowest.
  • Let the ball dig a tunnel up the middle, then bounce it over the wall into the space above.
  • Clip the very edge of the paddle to turn a straight shot into a sharp 60° angle toward the corner.
  • Every level makes the ball roughly 6% faster, so shorten your reactions as the numbers climb.
  • Losing a life is cheap — panicking and diving for an impossible return is what ends the run.
