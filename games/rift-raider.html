How to Play Rift Raider

You fly a scout ship through an asteroid rift that never stops scrolling. The field drifts left while your ship holds the left third of the screen, so nudge up and down with the arrow keys or WASD to slip between rocks, and drift left or right when the corridor opens. Your cannon fires automatically, so concentrate on dodging. Each run is measured in metres and points.

Raiders slide in from the right and shoot back; a plasma bolt or asteroid costs one of your 3 lives, though a shield capsule soaks one hit. Beyond rapid fire and the three-lane spread cannon, capsules now carry homing missiles, a piercing beam, a screen-clearing bomb and a spare life.

The first boss arrives at 720 metres, then a stronger one roughly every 1,000 metres. Normal raiders clear the lane for each duel, with a warning banner and a boss health bar. The Crimson Warden, Violet Hydra and Amber Tyrant alternate aimed volleys, radial spirals and side streams. At half health every boss enters phase two with denser fire, faster movement and an enrage shockwave. Boss health, speed, fire rate and damage climb with each fight.

Destroy a boss for a large bonus and a choice of three permanent upgrades: damage, fire rate, piercing shots, a shield charge or an extra life. Threat levels rise with distance, speeding the scroll and tightening spawns.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ or WS to fly up and down
  • ← → or AD to drift back and forward a little
  • Your cannon fires automatically — no fire button needed
  • After a boss falls, press 1, 2 or 3 (or click a card) to choose an upgrade
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume, P / Esc to pause, and the Reset button restarts

Tips for a longer run

  • Stay near the middle of the lane; the top and bottom walls hide rocks.
  • Small rocks break in one hit — shoot them to clear an escape route.
  • Save a bomb for a boss: it clears the plasma screen and shaves the health bar.
  • Track the boss's vertical sweep and hold its height so your bolts connect.
