How to Play Rift Raider

You are flying a scout ship through an asteroid rift that never stops scrolling. The whole field drifts leftwards past you while your ship holds the left third of the screen, so the challenge is all about threading the gaps: nudge up and down with the arrow keys or WASD to slip between tumbling rocks, and drift a little left or right when the corridor opens up. Your cannon fires on its own, so keep your hands on the flight controls and let the guns look after themselves. Your run is measured in metres travelled and points earned, and the HUD tracks both as you go.

It is not just debris out there. Angular raider ships slide in from the right and shoot back, so you will need to weave as well as aim — a clean hit from a plasma bolt or a solid asteroid costs one of your 3 lives and punishes you with a short wobble while your shields recover. Drifting capsules carry helpful systems: rapid fire, a spread cannon that covers three lanes at once and a shield that soaks up a single collision. The rift gets faster and busier the longer you survive, so a high score means flying the same distance with twice the traffic coming the other way.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ or WS to fly up and down
  • ← → or AD to drift back and forward a little
  • Your cannon fires automatically — no fire button needed
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume, P / Esc to pause, R to restart

Tips for a longer run

  • Stay near the middle of the lane. The top and bottom walls hide rocks until it is too late.
  • Shoot the small rocks rather than flying around them — they break in one hit and clear your escape route.
  • Grab every shield capsule you can. One free hit is worth far more than a few extra points.
  • Spread fire from a power-up shreds raider squadrons; use those seconds to push forward through the traffic.
  • After a crash you are briefly immune — use that moment to fly straight through the debris instead of dodging it.
