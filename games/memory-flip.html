How to Play Memory Flip

Memory Flip hides eight pairs of emoji behind a 4×4 grid of sixteen cards and challenges you to find every match from memory. Tap or click any card to turn it face up. Reveal a second card and the two are compared: if the emoji match, both cards stay face up and lock in place; if they do not, they flip back after a short pause, so slow down and remember what you saw. The round is complete once all eight pairs are matched, and the timer stops the moment the last pair locks.

Finish with fewer moves and less time to earn a higher score. Your result is calculated as score = max(0, 1200 − moves × 25 − seconds × 4), so every extra peek and every passing second costs you points. Your best score is saved in this browser, giving you a target to beat on your next round.

Controls

  • Click or Tap a card to flip it face up
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume the round
  • P or Esc to pause and resume, R to restart
  • On a phone: tap the cards and use the on-screen buttons for a new game or pause

Tips for a high score

  • Start by flipping cards near the middle — you see more of the board from the centre.
  • Say each emoji out loud or trace its position in your head; naming them helps recall.
  • When you miss, remember both cards, not just the one you were hunting for.
  • Correct pairs cost nothing extra, so use them to narrow down the rest of the grid.
  • Speed matters: the score drops 4 points for every second, so keep the flips flowing.
