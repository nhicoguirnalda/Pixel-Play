Stickman Sprint

How to Play Stickman Sprint

You are a lone neon stickman sprinting endlessly through a glowing city — the ground never stops scrolling and the pace only gets faster. Your runner never slows down, so your whole job is survival: read the obstacles coming in from the right, react at the right moment and cover as many metres as you can. Every metre is a point, and every coin you scoop up along the way is worth a tasty bonus.

The rooftops are never safe for long. Neon barriers rise from the ground and must be jumped, low energy beams hang across the path and must be slid under, and open chasms split the road and have to be cleared with a well-timed leap. You get one double jump per airtime, so a panicked early jump can still be rescued — but only once. Clip an obstacle and your stickman stumbles, tumbles in a cartoon spill and the run ends right there.

Controls

  • Space, ↑ or W to jump — press again in mid-air for a double jump
  • ↓ or S to slide under the low beams (hold the key to keep sliding)
  • Click or tap the game screen to jump; on a phone use the Slide and Jump buttons
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart, Space to start

Tips for a long run

  • Watch the horizon, not your runner. The next obstacle is always readable a second early if you look ahead.
  • Save the double jump for gaps — barriers and beams only ever need a single, well-timed move.
  • Slide early rather than late: the beam is wide, and a late slide clips its trailing edge.
  • Coin arcs trace safe jump lines. Follow them and the obstacles tend to line up neatly underneath.
  • Speed keeps climbing with distance, so a smooth rhythm beats panic mashing every time.

Scoring

Your score is the distance covered in metres plus 10 points for every coin collected. Distance climbs faster as the sprint accelerates, so a long run is worth far more than a lucky short one. Your best score is saved automatically in this browser, and the HUD keeps Score, Best, Distance and Coins live while you run.
