How to Play Pong Arena

Pong Arena is a head-to-head paddle duel against a computer opponent. A glowing ball bounces around a fenced court: you defend the left goal with the cyan paddle, the AI defends the right with the pink one. Miss the ball and your opponent scores. The first player to reach 7 points takes the match.

The real skill is in the angle. Where the ball lands on your paddle decides where it travels next — strike with the centre and it returns flat and predictable, catch it near the top or bottom edge and you fire it away at up to 60°, sending the AI scrambling. Every paddle hit also makes the ball a little faster, so long rallies turn into genuine tests of nerve. The AI is quick but not perfect: it reacts with a small delay and can be dragged out of position with sharp angles.

Controls

  • Move the mouse over the court to slide your paddle up and down
  • On a touchscreen, drag your finger along the court to steer the paddle
  • ↑ ↓ or WS to move the paddle manually
  • Space or a tap to serve, P or Esc to pause, R to restart

Tips for a winning streak

  • Aim with the edges of your paddle to open up steep return angles the AI cannot reach.
  • Return to the middle of the goal after every shot so you are ready for either side.
  • Watch the AI paddle, not the ball — it always drifts toward the last place it saw the ball.
  • Keep rallies short when you are ahead; the ball speeds up with every hit.
  • Your best win streak is saved in this browser. Win again to extend it — a loss resets it to zero.
