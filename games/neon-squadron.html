How to Play Neon Squadron

Neon Squadron is a vertical shoot-em-up. You fly a small starfighter pinned to the lower half of the screen while alien formations sweep in from the top. Your ship fires on its own, so your job is positioning: slide left and right to line up shots, ease upward for a better angle, and drop back down when the incoming fire gets thick. Every alien you destroy adds to your score.

Every fourth wave sends a boss, and three archetypes rotate in: the wide Bulwark Cruiser, the spinning Ring Warden and the aimed Lance Serpent. Each has a health bar, a faster second phase at half health and one big telegraphed attack — glowing lanes, a charging ring or a marked lance line — so you always get a moment to reach safety. Beat one and choose one of three upgrades with 1, 2 or 3 (or by clicking): extra damage, a faster fire rate, another shield charge, a screen bomb or an extra life.

Destroyed aliens drop eight rotating power-ups: rapid fire, spread shot, a shield, homing missiles, a piercing laser, a screen-clearing bomb, a magnet that pulls in drops, and a temporary double-score boost. Timed effects show their remaining time on the left; stored bombs wait until you press X (or tap the bomb chip) to clear enemy bullets and damage everything on screen.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to fly your ship
  • Space to fire (auto-fire is on, so holding it is optional)
  • X or B to spend a stored screen bomb
  • 1 2 3 to pick an upgrade after a boss
  • Space or Enter to start, Enter/P/Esc to pause, and the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad, tap cards to choose upgrades and tap the bomb chip

Tips for a higher score

  • Learn each boss's warning glow — heavy attacks are always announced before they fire.
  • Grab homing missiles or the laser before a boss so its health bar drops faster.
  • A magnet is worth taking even at full health: it sweeps up drops you would miss.
  • Save bombs for a wall of bullets, not for a single alien.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
