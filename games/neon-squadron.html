How to Play Neon Squadron

Neon Squadron is a vertical shoot-em-up. You fly a small starfighter pinned to the lower half of the screen while alien formations sweep in from the top. Your ship fires on its own, so your job is positioning: slide left and right to line up shots, ease upward for a better angle, and drop back down when the incoming fire gets thick. Every alien you destroy adds to your score.

Each cleared formation advances you to the next wave, and the waves get faster, denser and meaner. Every fifth wave is a boss — a huge armoured cruiser with a long health bar that sweeps across the top of the screen and answers with wide bullet spreads. Between the shooting, destroyed aliens sometimes drop rotating power-ups that drift downward: pick them up for rapid fire, a three-way spread shot or a shield that soaks one hit. They expire after a few seconds, so grab them while they last. You start with 3 lives; lose them all and the squadron is down.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to fly your ship
  • Space to fire (auto-fire is on, so holding it is optional)
  • Space or Enter to start, Enter or P or Esc to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad and the pause button below the field

Tips for a higher score

  • Stay around the middle of the field so you can dodge either way when a bullet spread arrives.
  • Save rapid fire for boss fights — the extra shot rate melts a health bar fast.
  • A shield is worth more than a life. Grab it before it drifts off the bottom of the screen.
  • Kill the aliens nearest the top first: their bullets reach you the slowest, so you get more warning.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
