How to Play Stickman Climb

Stickman Climb is a physics-based climber with just two buttons. Your little stickman hangs from a towering neon cliff by a pickaxe in each hand. Pressing ← hammers the left pickaxe up to a new hold, pressing → hammers the right one. Each swing yanks the body upward toward that hand while the other arm stretches and dangles, so alternating left and right builds a jarring, momentum-driven rhythm that slowly carries you up the rock face. Mashing both keys frees both hands — and sends the climber plummeting.

Your score is the height in metres you reach, shown live in the HUD together with your best climb, which is saved in this browser. There is no finish line and no summit flag: the cliff is endless, and you get exactly one life. Slip more than a few metres below your highest hold, or take a hit from the rockfall, and the run ends immediately with your height and best on the results card.

The higher you climb, the busier the wall gets. Every 100 metres opens a harder tier: loose rocks crumble under a pickaxe, overhangs force you to swing wide, sliding ledges shift, and gusts shove you off line. Watch for pulsing impact rings: after a short telegraph, a falling rock or a three-shard icicle volley slams into that marked height. Climb past the mark or hang still and let it shatter. Jagged red ledges are lethal — touch one and the climb ends instantly — and both hazards grow more frequent as you gain height.

Controls

  • ← or A to hammer the left pickaxe up
  • → or D to hammer the right pickaxe up
  • Space to start, Enter, P or Esc to pause, and the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone: tap the ◀ Left pick / Right pick ▶ buttons below the cliff

Tips for a tall climb

  • Alternate steadily. Rhythm beats panic — each clean swing gains a few metres.
  • Hang still below a falling shard; climbing into a marked height is how good runs end.
  • Bounce off jagged ledges and grab again quickly — they cost height, not a life, unless the fall is long.
  • Cross gust zones with quick swings and watch the wind gauge so the wind cannot drag you off the face.
