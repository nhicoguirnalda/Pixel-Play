How to Play Stickman Climb

Stickman Climb is a physics-based climber with just two buttons. Your little stickman hangs from a towering neon cliff by a pickaxe in each hand. Pressing ← hammers the left pickaxe up to a new hold, pressing → hammers the right one. Each swing yanks the body upward toward that hand while the other arm stretches and dangles, so alternating left and right builds a jarring, momentum-driven rhythm that slowly carries you up the rock face. Mashing both keys at once frees both hands — and sends the climber plummeting.

Your score is the height in metres you reach, shown live in the HUD together with your best climb, which is saved in this browser. You start with three lives and one clear goal: the summit flag at 80 metres. Fall too far below your last ledge and you lose a life, then respawn on that ledge ready to try again. The cliff is never empty. Jutting overhangs force you to swing out sideways, loose rocks flash with cracks for barely a second before they crumble out from under a pickaxe, every other ledge slides back and forth, and stronger gusts shove you sideways while the wind gauge shows which way they are blowing.

Controls

  • ← or A to hammer the left pickaxe up
  • → or D to hammer the right pickaxe up
  • Space to start, Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: tap the ◀ Left pick / Right pick ▶ buttons below the cliff

Tips for a tall climb

  • Alternate steadily. Rhythm beats panic — each clean swing gains a few metres.
  • Watch for flashing cracks: a loose rock gives you only a moment to shift your weight to the other arm.
  • Overhangs push you out from the wall; swing wide and let momentum carry you around the lip.
  • Land on sliding ledges to rest, reset your grip and set a new checkpoint — but do not linger, they travel further now.
  • Cross gust zones with quick swings and watch the wind gauge so the wind cannot drag you off the face.
