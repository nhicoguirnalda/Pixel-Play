How to Play Asteroid Storm

Asteroid Storm drops you into a 640×480 asteroid field in a small ship that keeps whatever momentum you give it. ← and → spin the ship, ↑ fires the thrusters and Space shoots. Because the ship drifts, the trick is to tap the thrusters in short bursts, let drag slow you down, and line up your next shot before you commit to a direction. Fly off one side of the screen and you appear on the other, so there is nowhere to hide and nowhere to crash.

Every asteroid you hit is worth points, but big rocks do not simply vanish — they break into two smaller, faster chunks, and those split again into quick little fragments. Clear every rock in the field and a fresh wave arrives with more asteroids and more speed. You begin with three lives: touching a rock costs one life and puts you back in the middle of the field with a short burst of protection. Lose them all and the run is over. Your best score is saved in this browser, so keep an eye on that number in the HUD.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to rotate the ship
  • ↑ or W to thrust forward, ↓ S to brake gently
  • Space to fire, P or Esc to pause, Enter to pause or resume, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the ⟲ / ▲ Thrust / 🔥 Fire / ⟳ buttons, or tap the arena to turn toward that spot

Tips for a high score

  • Big rocks split into two, so break them at the edge of the screen where the pieces drift away from you.
  • Fire in short taps rather than holding the button — your shots travel faster than the rocks.
  • Never sit still in the middle. The centre is where fragments from every direction end up.
  • Use the wrap-around edges to escape: a quick dash off the top reappears at the bottom.
  • Keep your last life safe by saving a clear lane before you break the final big rock of a wave.
