Daily Word

How to Play Daily Word

Daily Word is a calm, endless five-letter guessing puzzle. A hidden word is picked for the round and you have six guesses to find it. Type any valid five-letter word on your physical keyboard — or tap the on-screen keyboard drawn right inside the game board — then press Enter to lock the row in. Each guess is graded letter by letter: a green tile means that letter is correct and in the right spot, a yellow tile means the letter exists but sits in the wrong position, and a grey tile means the letter is not in the word at all. The colours flip in with a smooth animation, and the keyboard at the bottom recolours itself so you always know which letters are still worth trying.

Watch out for repeated letters. If the hidden word contains a letter twice and you only guess it once, only one tile can be graded as present — the game resolves duplicates properly so a single guess can never reveal more copies of a letter than the word actually holds. Solve the puzzle and the score is 700 points minus 100 for every guess past the first, so a hole-in-one is worth the full 700. Run out of six guesses and the word is revealed so you always learn the answer. Every new round draws a fresh word, and the game avoids repeating the previous answer so no two rounds in a row feel the same.

Controls

  • Type A–Z on your keyboard to fill the current row
  • Backspace erases the last letter, Enter submits the row
  • Esc or Shift+P pauses the game — Enter never pauses, it only submits
  • On a phone: tap the letters, ⌫ and ↵ keys drawn on the board itself
  • Space starts a new round from the title screen

Tips for a strong streak

  • Open with a vowel-rich word such as AUDIO or CRANE to map the board fast.
  • Grey letters matter: mentally cross them out of every future guess.
  • A yellow letter you have not placed yet is your best next-move clue.
  • Duplicate tiles are common — a word like SPEED hides a repeat you must plan for.
  • Your best score and current streak are saved in this browser automatically.
