Turret Guard

How to Play Turret Guard

Robots march a winding road toward your base and only your turret stands in their way. Aim with the mouse or a finger and hold to fire; the barrel follows your cursor and hits spark off robot plating. Kills earn points and credits, but any robot that reaches the gate damages the base's 20 hit points.

The escalation is sharp. Scouts, crawlers and tanks gain 16% health per wave and accelerate up to 56 px/s faster than their base speed, while spawns close in to one every 0.26 seconds. From wave eight, every fourth robot is a gold-ringed veteran with 55% more health, 15% more speed and an extra point of gate damage, and all minions gain damage every five waves. Your turret starts at 0.75 damage, so every upgrade rank matters.

Every fifth wave sends an Armoured Walker with a health bar. Walkers start at 190 HP, move faster and deal 12 gate damage. Health gains about 85% per tier plus 7% per wave; speed and damage climb with both. At half health it enters phase two, accelerates 35% and summons 2–5 minions, more often later. A wave-15 walker carries over 1,200 HP; a wave-20 walker is a wall.

Six upgrades sit in two rows at the bottom-left (buttons below the field on phones). Fire rate and Damage are the core; Splash hits nearby robots, Pierce passes through extra targets, Slow field chills the approach, and Repair restores 8 HP. Cleared waves pay more, and walker kills grant bonus credits plus a small repair. The HUD's threat tier tracks the escalating pressure.

Controls

  • Move the mouse to aim — the turret follows your cursor
  • Hold the left mouse button (or a finger) to fire, release to stop
  • Click a panel or press 1–6: fire rate, damage, splash, pierce, slow field, repair
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume, P / Esc to pause, the Reset button restarts

Tips for holding the line

  • Crawlers reach the gate first — aim at the most advanced robot, not the closest.
  • Buy Pierce before a boss wave; walkers often have escorts in front.
  • Gold-ringed veterans are worth extra score and credits; finish them early.
  • Keep repair credits spare before every fifth wave.
