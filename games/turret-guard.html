How to Play Turret Guard

Rows of marching robots are walking a winding road straight toward your base, and you have a single defence turret to stop them. Point at the road with the mouse or your finger and hold to fire: the barrel always swings to face your cursor, and every hit sparks off the robot's plating. Everything you destroy adds points to your score and credits to your repair fund. Nothing gets past the fence for free — any robot that reaches the base takes a chunk out of its 20 hit points. When the last of them drains away, the defence falls and the run is over.

The waves get heavier as you go. Wave one is a squad of light scouts, but crawlers sprint down the road from wave three and squat armoured tanks start trundling along from wave two, soaking up far more punishment. Spending credits is the answer: the Fire rate upgrade shortens the gap between shots and the Damage upgrade makes each round hit harder. Two big upgrade buttons sit in the bottom-left corner of the field — click them whenever you can afford them, or use the matching buttons under the field on a phone. Between waves there is a short pause: reposition your aim, buy an upgrade and get ready for the next push.

Controls

  • Move the mouse over the field to aim — the turret follows your cursor
  • Hold the left mouse button (or a finger) to fire, release to stop
  • Click the ⚡ Fire rate and 💥 Damage panels on the field to buy upgrades
  • 1 and 2 buy those upgrades from the keyboard
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume, P / Esc to pause, R to restart

Tips for holding the line

  • Big robots walk the long way around, so shoot the fast crawlers first — leaks hurt more than slow kills.
  • Aim at the corners of the road. That is where the walkers bunch up and a burst of fire hits several at once.
  • Buy fire rate early and damage later: more bullets stop a rush, more power stops a tank.
  • Watch the road, not your crosshair — the turret aims itself, your job is to pick the target.
  • Keep a few credits spare before a wave ends so you can upgrade the moment the bonus lands.
