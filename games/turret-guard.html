How to Play Turret Guard

Marching robots walk a winding road toward your base, and you have one turret to stop them. Point with the mouse or a finger and hold to fire: the barrel swings to face your cursor, and every hit sparks off the robot's plating. Kills add points and credits to your repair fund. Any robot that reaches the gate takes a chunk out of the base's 20 hit points. When the base runs dry, the defence falls.

The waves get heavier as you go. Robot health grows by about 13% per wave, their legs move a little faster, and spawns arrive closer together. Crawlers sprint down the road from wave three and armoured tanks soak up far more punishment. Every fifth wave is a boss wave: an Armoured Walker with a health bar across the top of the field. At half health it enters a second phase, speeds up and starts summoning pairs of minions, with a pulsing warning ring and a short countdown. Its health scales with the wave, so late walkers need real focus.

Six upgrades sit in two rows at the bottom-left of the field (buttons under the field on a phone). Fire rate and Damage work as before; Splash makes impacts damage nearby robots, Pierce lets rounds pass through extra targets, Slow field creates a chilled zone in front of the base, and Repair restores 6 base HP. Click a panel or press 1 to 6. Between waves there is a short pause: reposition your aim, buy an upgrade and get ready for the next push.

Controls

  • Move the mouse over the field to aim — the turret follows your cursor
  • Hold the left mouse button (or a finger) to fire, release to stop
  • Click a panel or press 1–6: fire rate, damage, splash, pierce, slow field, repair
  • Space or Enter to start, pause or resume, P / Esc to pause, R to restart

Tips for holding the line

  • Fast crawlers reach the gate first — aim at the most advanced robot, not the closest one.
  • Buy a rank of Pierce before a boss wave; the walker often has escorts in front of it.
  • Keep 12 credits spare once the base is scratched so a Repair is always available.
