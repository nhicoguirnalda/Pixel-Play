How to Play Defuse Squad

Defuse Squad is a first-person raid in three escalating rounds. Each round you spawn somewhere in a maze-like training compound, and somewhere inside it a glowing bomb site pulses on the minimap. Reach the marked site, then hold F (or E) to defuse — the progress bar fills in about three seconds, but release the key and it decays slowly. Clear all three rounds to win the run.

Defusing is never free. The moment a round begins, defending bots activate and converge on your position, and they spawn faster while you are on the defuse key. You can release, sweep them out with your blaster, then resume — that stop-and-go rhythm is the core of the game. A 45-second bomb timer runs the whole round, so time spent hiding is time the site has left. If the timer hits zero, or your HP runs out, the round is lost and the run ends. Rounds 2 and 3 send more bots, faster, with tougher plating; between rounds you repair some HP and refill your magazine.

Controls

  • WASD to move and strafe; ← → to turn the view
  • Click the view once to lock the mouse, then move it to look around — Esc releases it
  • Space to jump — hop over incoming orb fire while closing on the site
  • Z or left click to fire — the blaster reloads itself after a short pause
  • Hold F or E at the glowing site to defuse the bomb
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the run

Tips for clearing rounds

  • Strafe constantly while defusing — bots aim where you were, not where you are.
  • Use corners and crates as funnels; bots line up nicely down a corridor.
  • The minimap shows your view cone, the site waypoint and every bot on the radar.
  • Let the bar decay is fine — a half-finished defuse still saves you seconds later.

Scoring

  • Every bot you disable pays more in later rounds; round-clear bonuses scale with the time left on the bomb timer.
  • Shots fired versus shots landed tracks your accuracy, shown on the end screen.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
