How to Play Wavebreak

Wavebreak drops you into a neon arena with a right-hand viewmodel, a health bar and endless drone waves. The gun tracks your reticle, rising when you aim up and dropping when you aim down. WASD moves, the mouse or ← → turns, ↑ ↓ aims, F or a click fires, and Space jumps. Clear a wave, catch your breath, then do it again.

Three guns, three rhythms. The Blaster holds 14 rounds with a 0.16-second cycle and one-second reload. The Rifle hits for 2.6 base damage but holds only 8 rounds, cycling in 0.44 seconds and reloading in 1.55. The SMG sprays 26 rounds every 0.085 seconds, trading punch for speed. Each gun keeps its own ammo and reload timer, so switching never refills. A wave-clear tops up all three magazines. Press 1 2 3 during a wave, Q to cycle, or tap the on-screen Weapon button; an on-canvas panel shows the active gun and ammo.

Every kill pays credits for the between-wave upgrade menu: 1 for faster fire, 2 for +1 damage, 3 for armor and repairs. During the breather those keys buy upgrades instead of switching guns. Upgrades stack. Kills also build a combo multiplier — chain them quickly and your score explodes. The run ends at zero HP; your best score is saved.

Drones hover at different heights, so the reticle must sit on the drone itself. Point-blank shots deal about 1.65× damage, long shots 0.5×, so close the gap and watch the range readout.

Controls

  • WASD move and strafe; ← → turn; ↑ ↓ aim
  • Click to lock the mouse; move to look and aim; Esc releases
  • Space jumps; F or left click fires; empty guns reload themselves
  • 1 2 3 switch guns during a wave; Q or the Weapon button cycles
  • 1 2 3 buy upgrades during the breather
  • Enter, P or Esc pauses; R restarts

Tips

  • Keep crates between you and the swarm.
  • Never stand still; circling the arena centre keeps the orbs missing.
  • Rifle for heavies, SMG for runners, Blaster at mid-range.
  • Two early damage upgrades beat one level of everything.

Scoring

  • Each drone pays credits plus a wave bonus; combo boosts score, not credits.
  • Combo rises with quick kills and resets after a couple of seconds without one.
