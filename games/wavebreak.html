How to Play Wavebreak

Wavebreak drops you into a neon arena with a blaster, a health bar and endless waves of attack drones. Every wave adds more drones, and they come faster and tougher. WASD moves you around the crates, the mouse or ← → turns the view, ↑ ↓ raises and lowers the reticle, and F or a click fires. Tap Space to jump over incoming shots. Clear a wave, catch your breath, then do it again.

Every kill pays credits, and credits are the whole metagame. Between waves a three-second breather opens the upgrade menu: 1 for a faster fire rate, 2 for more damage, or 3 for armor that raises maximum HP and repairs you. Upgrades stack, but prices climb, so choose between speed, power and survival. Kills also build a combo multiplier — chain them quickly and your score explodes. The run ends at zero HP, and your best score is saved.

Distance changes everything. Drones hover at different heights, so the reticle must sit on the drone itself. Shots hit hardest up close: point-blank damage is triple a long-range shot, so close the gap and check the range readout beneath the crosshair.

Controls

  • WASD to move and strafe; ← → to turn
  • Click the view to lock the mouse, then move it to look and aim up/down — Esc releases it
  • ↑ ↓ or the ▲ ▼ buttons to aim without the mouse
  • Space to jump clear of drone fire
  • F or left click to fire; the blaster reloads itself
  • 1 2 3 to buy upgrades during the breather (or click them)
  • Enter, P or Esc pauses; R restarts

Tips for lasting longer

  • Keep crates between you and the swarm — drones line up down a lane.
  • Never stand still; circling the arena centre keeps the orbs missing.
  • Get close before firing: point-blank shots deal about 1.6× damage, long shots 0.5×.
  • Two early damage upgrades beat one level of everything — fewer shots means fewer hits.
  • The minimap shows your view cone and every drone; watch it for flanks.

Scoring

  • Each drone pays base credits plus a wave bonus; the combo multiplier boosts score, not credits.
  • Combo rises with quick kills and resets after a couple of seconds without one.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
