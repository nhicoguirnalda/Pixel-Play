How to Play Wavebreak

Wavebreak drops you into a neon arena with a blaster, a health bar and endless waves of attack drones. Every wave adds more drones, and they come faster and tougher than the last. You WASD around the crates and pillars, turn with the mouse or the arrow keys, and fire with F or a click. Tap Space to jump over incoming drone shots as you dodge. Clear a wave, catch your breath, then do it again until the swarm finally puts you down.

Every kill pays credits, and credits are the whole metagame. Between waves you get a three-second breather with an upgrade menu on screen: press 1 for a faster fire rate, 2 for more damage per shot, or 3 to bolt on armor plating that raises your maximum HP and repairs you. Each upgrade stacks, but the price climbs with every level, so choose between speed, power and survival carefully. Kills also build a combo multiplier — chain kills quickly and your score explodes. The run ends when your HP hits zero, and your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • WASD to move and strafe; ← → to turn the view
  • Click the view once to lock the mouse, then move it to look around — Esc releases it
  • Space to jump — leap clear of drone fire while you reposition
  • F or left click to fire — the blaster reloads itself after a short pause
  • 1 2 3 to buy upgrades during the breather (or click them)
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the run

Tips for lasting longer

  • Keep the crates between you and the swarm — drones line up nicely down a lane.
  • Never stand still; circling the arena centre keeps the orbs missing.
  • Two levels of damage early beats one level of everything: fewer shots per drone means fewer hits on you.
  • The minimap shows your view cone and every drone on the radar — watch it for flanks.
  • Space skips the breather when you feel ready.

Scoring

  • Each drone pays base credits plus a wave bonus; the combo multiplier multiplies your score, not your credits.
  • Combo rises with quick consecutive kills and resets after a couple of seconds without one.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
