How to Play Wavebreak

Wavebreak drops you into a neon arena with a forward-pointing right-hand viewmodel, a health bar and endless drone waves. The gun rises and falls with your reticle. WASD moves, the mouse or ← → turns, ↑ ↓ aims, F or a click fires, and Space jumps. Clear a wave and catch your breath.

Three guns, three rhythms. The Blaster holds 14 rounds, the Rifle hits for 2.6 damage with 8, and the SMG sprays 26 fast rounds. Each keeps its own ammo and reload timer, so switching never refills; a wave-clear tops up all magazines. Press R to reload early. Press 1 2 3, Q to cycle, or tap the on-screen Weapon button.

Every fifth wave is a boss fight. The Overseer and its later forms arrive with a warning banner, a health bar and escalating attacks: radial orbs, aimed volleys and drone summons. At half health it enters phase two, firing faster with wider patterns. The wave clears only when the boss dies. Between bosses the threat tier keeps climbing, adding shielded Bulwarks, strafing Stingers and telegraphed Snipers. Enemy health, speed, damage and spawn cadence rise every wave, so each fight is harder than the last.

Every kill pays credits for the between-wave upgrade menu: 1 faster fire, 2 +1 damage, 3 armor and repairs. Point-blank shots deal about 1.65× damage; long shots 0.5×. Kills build a combo multiplier. The run ends at zero HP.

Controls

  • WASD move and strafe; ← → turn; ↑ ↓ aim
  • Click to lock the mouse; move to look and aim; Esc releases
  • Space jumps; F or left click fires; empty guns reload themselves
  • 1 2 3 switch guns during a wave; Q or the Weapon button cycles
  • 1 2 3 buy upgrades during the breather
  • Enter, P or Esc pauses; R reloads, the Reset button restarts

Tips

  • Keep crates between you and the swarm.
  • Never stand still; circling the arena centre keeps the orbs missing.
  • Flank shielded Bulwarks, strafe against Stingers, and save a full magazine for the boss.
  • Two early damage upgrades beat one level of everything.

Scoring

  • Each drone pays credits plus a wave bonus; combo boosts score, not credits.
  • Combo rises with quick kills and resets after a couple of seconds without one.
