How to Play Merge 2048

Merge 2048 is the classic sliding-tile puzzle played on a 4×4 board. Every move nudges all sixteen squares in one direction at once: tiles glide as far as they can go, and two tiles showing the same number combine into one tile worth double. A pair can only merge once per move, so the order in which you slide matters. After each move that changes the board a brand-new tile appears — usually a 2, occasionally a 4 — and the board fills up quickly if you drift without a plan.

Your score grows by the value of every tile you create, so merging two 512 tiles adds 1024 points in a single move. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match, leaving you with no legal move. The goal is to build the legendary 2048 tile, and you may keep playing afterwards to chase 4096 and beyond. Your best score is saved in this browser, so there is always a target to beat.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to slide the tiles
  • Space to start, pause or resume, P to pause, and the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone: swipe across the board in the direction you want, or use the on-screen pad

Tips for a high score

  • Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile parked there for the whole run.
  • Use only two directions most of the time; every extra direction scrambles your layout.
  • Keep the top row sorted in descending order so merges happen where you expect.
  • Never fill the fourth row unless you have a merge ready — that is how boards die.
  • Plan two moves ahead: a slide that creates a gap is often worth more than a quick merge.
