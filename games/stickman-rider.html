How to Play Stickman Rider

Stickman Rider is a side-view motorbike trials game across nine hand-built trails that get meaner with every gate. You ride a bouncy trials bike over rougher hills, rocky bumps, fallen logs and open chasms, and your job is simple: reach the checkered finish gate before the clock empties. The clock tightens, the kickers launch higher and the trails climb harder, so smooth throttle control matters more than raw speed — the bike has real suspension bounce, wheel grip and mid-air rotation.

New on the later trails are swinging log pendulums. Each timber gantry hangs a heavy log over the track, marked by a red sweep on the ground and a SWING sign. The log swings faster each level, so watch its arc, let it come toward you, then cross behind it. A clip knocks the rider off, costs a life and restarts the level.

You crash when the rider's head touches the ground. A ring of cartoon stars pops, a life is spent and the level restarts — three crashes end the run, but banked score is kept. Finish a trail to earn a clear bonus of 500 points plus 150 for each level reached plus 10 points per remaining second. Grab the glowing coins floating along the route for 50 points each, and land a full 360° flip for a 250-point FLIP! bonus that multiplies for double and triple flips.

Controls

  • ↑ or W to open the throttle, ↓ or S to brake, then reverse once you stop
  • ← to lean back (wheelies on the ground, backflips in the air)
  • → to lean forward — hold it mid-jump to rotate into a front flip
  • Space to start, Enter/P/Esc to pause, the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone: use the ◀ Lean / 🛞 Brake / 🔥 Throttle / Lean ▶ buttons under the course

Tips for a clean run

  • Lean back as you hit a ramp — a lifted front wheel keeps the landing rear-wheel first.
  • Tap the throttle in the air: it barely accelerates you, but landing nose-up saves the run.
  • Chasms punish hesitation. Build speed early, then hold the throttle across the gap.
  • Time each swinging log: wait for it to pass, then commit through the gantry.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
