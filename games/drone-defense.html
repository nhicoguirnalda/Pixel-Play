How to Play Drone Defense

Drone Defense drops your little robot in the middle of a walled neon arena. Rogue drones pour in from every edge and steer straight for you, so there is nowhere to hide — keep moving. It is a top-down twin-stick battle: WASD drives the robot while the mouse swings your cannon. Your blaster fires on its own toward the cursor, so all you do is aim and strafe. You start with 100 HP.

Destroyed drones sometimes drop a coloured pickup; walk over it to collect. Pink is rapid fire, cyan is a three-way shot, blue is a shield that absorbs one hit, green repairs 30 health and purple slows every drone and boss for a few seconds.

Clear a wave and pick one of three permanent upgrades with 1, 2 or 3 — or by clicking a card. Choose extra damage, a faster trigger, more movement speed, +20 max HP with a repair, or bullets that pierce an extra drone. Every fifth wave a huge armoured warden arrives with its own health bar. It fires aimed volleys and expanding rings, summons minions, and switches to a faster second phase at half health. When it starts charging, a growing red ring marks its burst; stand in one of the gaps or get clear.

Controls

  • WASD or the arrow keys to drive your robot around the arena
  • Move the mouse to aim — the blaster fires on its own toward the cursor, and holding the mouse button fires too
  • 1 2 3 to choose a permanent upgrade between waves
  • Space or Enter to start, Enter/P/Esc to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad to move and drag a finger on the arena to aim

Tips for surviving longer

  • Never stop moving. A stationary robot is a magnet for every drone on the field.
  • Circle the outer wall instead of cutting through the middle — it strings the swarm out behind you.
  • Collect green repairs early; a full health bar is worth more than a risky detour later.
  • Against the warden, keep your distance and slide sideways when the red charge ring appears.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
