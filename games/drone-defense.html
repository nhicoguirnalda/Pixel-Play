How to Play Drone Defense

Drone Defense drops your little robot in the middle of a walled neon arena. Rogue drones pour in from every edge of the screen and steer straight for you, so there is nowhere to hide — you have to keep moving. It is a top-down twin-stick style battle: WASD drives the robot while the mouse swings your cannon around. Your blaster fires on its own toward wherever the cursor is pointing, so all you really do is aim and strafe. You start with 100 HP and the health bar in the HUD is the only thing keeping you in the game.

Every drone that touches you costs health and knocks you backwards, but a short burst of invulnerability follows each hit so you can slip away instead of being shredded on the spot. Drones come in a few flavours: basic green chasers, quick cyan scouts that sprint at you, and heavy orange tanks that take several shots to crack but score the most points. Clear a whole wave and the next one arrives bigger, faster and tougher — but your repair drones patch up a little health for every wave you finish, so clean clears pay off. Keep circling the arena, keep your crosshair ahead of the swarm, and see how many waves you can survive.

Controls

  • WASD or the arrow keys to drive your robot around the arena
  • Move the mouse to aim — the blaster fires on its own toward the cursor, and holding the mouse button fires too
  • Space or Enter to start, Enter or P or Esc to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the on-screen direction pad to move and drag a finger on the arena to aim

Tips for surviving longer

  • Never stop moving. A stationary robot is a magnet for every drone on the field.
  • Circle the outer wall instead of cutting through the middle — it strings the swarm out behind you.
  • Deal with cyan scouts first. They close the distance fastest and hurt your escape routes.
  • Save the bulky orange tanks for last; they move slowly and you can kite them while you thin the pack.
  • Each wave you clear repairs a little health, so finishing a swarm cleanly is worth playing safe for.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
