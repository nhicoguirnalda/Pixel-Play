How to Play Pin Escape

Pin Escape is a pull-the-pin logic puzzle. Each level is a single-screen scene: your stickman waits in one chamber, a treasure chest sits in another, and between them are compartments full of gold, lava, rushing water, rolling boulders — and the occasional friendly shield or ladder. Everything is held back by metal pins with big ring handles. Click or tap a pin to slide it out, and whatever it was holding drops straight down. The order you pull them in is everything: drop the gold onto the safe floor first, then open the stickman's door so he can walk across without a hazard landing on his head.

Released contents obey simple physics: they fall until something stops them, and liquids spread along whatever floor they land on. So a wrong pin can flood the walkway with lava or send a boulder rolling straight at your little man. If a hazard touches him, the level fails and resets — pull the pins in the right order and he walks to the chest, the lid flies open and you advance. Clear a level first try for three stars, after one or two retries for two stars, and one star otherwise. Your star total per level is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • Click or tap a pin to pull it out
  • ← → to cycle the selected pin, Space to pull the selected one
  • Number keys 1–9 pull that pin directly (left to right, top to bottom)
  • Enter, P or Esc to pause, R to restart the level
  • On a phone: tap pins on the screen, or use the ◀ Pin / 📌 Pull / Pin ▶ buttons

Tips for tricky levels

  • Look at where each pin's contents will land before you pull anything.
  • Liquids flow as soon as they land — route them into pits, never onto the walkway.
  • A shield saves your stickman from one hazard hit, so grab it before danger.
  • Ladders bridge gaps; drop them into the pit before your man reaches the edge.
  • Some pins are traps that look useful. If in doubt, pull the glowing hinted pin first.
