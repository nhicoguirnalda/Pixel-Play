Word Fall

How to Play Word Fall

Word Fall is a typing defence game. Words rain down from the top of the arena in six lanes while a glowing defence line waits at the bottom. Just type each word on your physical keyboard — every letter you press lands in the typed buffer bar above the line, and the moment the buffer spells a falling word exactly, hit Enter to send it shattering into sparks. Words that reach the line cost you a life, and you only have three, shown as hearts in the HUD and beside the line.

The arena reads your mind before your fingers catch up: any falling word the buffer could still become gets a thin underline, so you always know which words are still in play. Type a letter that no underlined word can continue and the buffer shakes red — press Backspace to peel it off, or just press Enter to try anyway, which costs 25 points if the buffer matches nothing on screen. Empty sends are free, so never be afraid to clear a dead-end prefix and start a word again.

Controls

  • Type letters to fill the buffer, Backspace erases, Enter sends the word
  • Esc always pauses — P too, whenever no falling word needs that letter — and Space starts or resumes
  • R restarts from the pause screen or the game-over screen
  • On a phone: tap the arena to raise your keyboard — a hidden text box keeps it open while you type
  • Use the ⌫ Erase and ↵ Send buttons under the arena when tapping is easier than typing

Scoring and tips

  • Shattered words pay by length: 30 for three letters, 60 for four, 100 for five, 160 for six and 250 for seven or more
  • Every point is multiplied by the current wave, so long words deep into a run are worth a fortune
  • Clear a whole wave of eight words without letting one land and earn a 150-point perfect wave bonus
  • Chase the underlined words: shattering one instantly frees the buffer for your next target
  • Waves speed up and spawn faster over time — early accuracy beats late panic
