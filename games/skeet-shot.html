How to Play Skeet Shot

Skeet Shot is an arcade shooting range with a very simple rule: clay discs fly, you knock them down. Targets launch from the bottom of the range in pairs, arcing upward and drifting apart, then curve back down under gravity. Move the mouse to sweep your crosshair across the sky and click — or tap on a touchscreen — to fire at exactly where you are aiming.

You carry two shells per pair of clays, followed by a short reload. You have three lives: every clay that escapes unbroken costs one, and at zero the range closes immediately. Break a disc and your combo multiplier climbs; fire a shell into empty sky and it resets to one, so wild spraying is expensive. A round is 10 launches, and the speed, angle and drift of every pair changes, so no two sequences look alike. At the end of the final round every hit you landed is totalled up, along with an accuracy bonus for shots that found a target. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • Move the mouse to aim — the crosshair follows your pointer
  • Click, or tap the range on a touchscreen, to fire a shell
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume
  • P or Esc to pause, and the Reset button restarts the round

Tips for a higher score

  • Aim at the flat face of a disc, not its thin edge — lead the target slightly as it climbs.
  • The fastest clays travel furthest. Take the close, slow one first and use your second shell on the hard one.
  • Two shells means two chances: never spend both on the same place in the sky.
  • Protect your combo. One careful hit beats a rushed shot that breaks the streak.
  • Finish every round with shells to spare — the accuracy bonus rewards patience.
