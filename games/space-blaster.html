How to Play Space Blaster

You pilot a small defence ship at the bottom of a 600×600 starfield. Waves of alien formations glide in from the top of the screen, sweeping left and right as they descend, and dropping glowing bolts of their own. Your blaster fires straight up, so you win by lining up with a column and clearing it before the formation drifts away. Every alien you destroy is worth points, and clearing a whole wave pays a bonus before the next one arrives faster and lower.

You start with three lives, shown in the HUD. You lose one whenever an alien touches your ship or slips past the bottom edge of the arena — so never let the formation settle over you. After a hit your ship flickers for a moment and you are briefly invulnerable, which is the perfect window to reposition. The game ends when all three lives are gone, and your best score is saved in this browser so there is always a number to beat.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to slide the ship sideways
  • ↑ ↓ or WS to move up and down the arena (optional — use it carefully)
  • Space to fire, P or Esc to pause, Enter to pause or resume, R to restart
  • On a phone: tap the on-screen ◀ / 🔥 Fire / ▶ buttons, or tap a spot in the arena to steer there

Tips for a high score

  • Fire as you move — a steady stream of shots keeps the lowest row from ever reaching you.
  • Clear one column completely, then slide under the gap. Aliens cannot shoot what is not there.
  • Save the middle of the arena for emergencies; the corners are the safest places to wait.
  • Watch the bolts, not the aliens. Enemy shots are slow and easy to sidestep if you see them early.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
