How to Play Neon Blocks

Neon Blocks is a block-stacking classic played on a 10×20 well. One glowing shape falls at a time — seven different pieces in all, from the four-square straight bar to the square, the plus, the two zig-zags and the pair of little corners, each in its own neon colour. Slide a piece sideways, rotate it until it fits, then drop it onto the stack. Fill every cell of a horizontal row and that row flashes and vanishes. Clear four rows in a single drop for the biggest score in the game.

A preview shows the next piece, and a ghost copy of the current one marks exactly where it will land. Press C to bench a piece in the hold box and swap it back later — but you can only hold once per drop. Every 10 lines you clear, gravity speeds up, so tidy stacks beat tall towers: the run ends the moment new pieces can no longer enter the well. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to slide the piece across the well
  • ↑, X or W to rotate, ↓ or S to soft-drop gently
  • Space to hard-drop the piece (and to start a game), C or Shift to hold it
  • P, Esc or Enter to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the ◀ / ⟳ / ▼ / ⤓ / Hold / ▶ buttons under the well

Scoring and the scoreboard

A single line scores 100 points, a double 300, a triple 500 and a four-line clear 800 — all multiplied by your level. Soft-dropping adds 1 point per row, hard-dropping 2, so aggressive play pays. Levels rise every 10 lines, and the HUD keeps Score, Best, Lines and Level live; your best score survives restarts.

Tips for a high score

  • Keep the stack flat. A level floor means almost every piece fits somewhere useful.
  • Reserve one column — usually the far right — for the long straight bar and four-line clears.
  • Line the ghost up before you commit.
  • Use hold to park an awkward piece and bring it back when the stack suits it.
  • Speed climbs every 10 lines, so start clearing early instead of digging later.
