How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Take it in turns to drop your mark into one of the nine squares. You are ❌ X, the opponent is ⭕ O, and X always moves first. Line up three of your marks in a row — across, down or diagonally — and you take the round. Fill all nine squares with no line and it's a draw. Play against the machine and climb seven levels, or switch to two players and share the screen with a friend.

Controls

  • Click / Tap an empty square to place your mark
  • 🤖 vs AI and 👥 2 players switch the mode; the HUD shows your current AI level
  • Space or Enter starts a new round, and the Reset button restarts the campaign at level 1

How the seven AI levels work

Level 1 blunders on most turns, picking a random square. Each win moves you up a level and makes those blunders rarer; level 7 never slips. From there it plays minimax: it tries every empty square in turn, then imagines the best reply you could make, then the best reply to that reply, all the way to the end of the game. Finished lines score +10 for a win, 0 for a draw and −10 for a loss, and the bot picks whichever square gives it the best score even if you answer perfectly from then on. That search-and-backtrack loop makes level 7 literally unbeatable: the best a flawless human can force is a draw. Which is exactly the goal — draw, don't lose.

Tips for drawing or winning

  • Open with the centre square. It belongs to four of the eight possible winning lines, twice as many as any corner.
  • Always answer a threat. If the bot has two in a row with the third square open, block it before doing anything else.
  • Build forks. A fork is one square that creates two threats at once — the opponent can only block one, so the other wins the game.
  • Win to climb a level, lose and you drop one; draws hold your place. On the early levels punish the random moves by finishing your lines fast.
  • Your current win streak against the AI is saved in this browser — the HUD's Best shows it.
