How to Play Bubble Burst

Bubble Burst is a classic bubble shooter. A honeycomb wall of coloured bubbles fills the top of the board and you stand at the bottom with a launcher. Move the mouse or your finger to aim, and an aim guide will bounce off the side walls to show you exactly where your bubble is heading. Click or tap to fire the coloured bubble that is loaded, and it snaps into the first free slot it touches.

Three or more bubbles of the same colour joined together burst and award 10 points each. Anything left dangling below a burst is no longer attached to the ceiling, so the whole clump drops for a 20-point bonus per bubble — that is where the big scores come from. A fresh row creeps down every eight shots, so keep clearing space. If the wall reaches the danger line the round is over. Empty the board completely and you advance a level with more colours and a deeper starting wall.

Controls

  • Move the mouse or drag your finger to aim — the guide bounces off the walls
  • Click or tap to fire the loaded bubble
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume
  • P or Escape to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: use the on-screen Fire and Pause buttons below the board

Tips for a high score

  • Shoot the colours that already sit high on the wall — popping near the ceiling drops the most bubbles.
  • Aim at a bubble whose opposite side is crowded; the burst will cut a whole clump free.
  • Bounce shots off the side walls to reach awkward pockets you cannot hit straight on.
  • Watch the next-bubble preview and plan two shots ahead, not one.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
