Mine Field

How to Play Mine Field

Mine Field hides 12 bombs inside a 10×10 grid — that's 100 squares, and only 88 of them are safe. Your first tap is always a lucky one: the mines are scattered after you open your very first square, and that square plus its eight neighbours are guaranteed to be clear, so every game begins with a fair opening. Uncover all 88 safe squares and you clear the field; touch a mine and the round ends in an instant.

The numbers are the whole game. A number tells you exactly how many mines sit in the squares touching it, counting all eight directions — the diagonals included. A 3 sitting beside three closed squares means all three of them are bombs, so plant your flags there. A blank square means nothing dangerous is nearby at all, which is why the board ripples open around it: blank squares trigger a flood fill that instantly reveals every connected empty square and the numbered ring around its edge. From there it is pure logic — no guessing required in most rounds.

Controls

  • Click / Tap a square to reveal what is under it
  • Right-click a square to plant or remove a 🚩 flag
  • On a phone: switch on 🚩 Flag mode below the board so taps place flags instead of revealing, or press and hold a square for half a second to flag it
  • Space or Enter to start, R to shuffle a fresh grid

Tips for clearing the field

  • Open a corner first. Corner squares have only three neighbours instead of eight, so the early numbers give tighter, more reliable deductions.
  • Work along the edge of the opened area. Those numbers constrain the fewest hidden squares and are always the easiest to solve next.
  • A number that already "sees" its full count of flags is finished — every other square around it must be safe, so click them without fear.
  • Flags block the flood fill, just like in the classic game. Unflag a square if you need the reveal to pass through it.
  • Speed pays: your score is 1200 − 8 × seconds, with a floor of 100 points, and your best result is saved automatically in this browser.
