How to Play Word Link

Word Link hands you a circle of glowing letter tiles and a board of empty word slots. Every word you need can be spelled using only the letters in the ring, using each tile no more than once — and every tile in the ring belongs to at least one answer. Tap or click letters in order to build a word in the answer bar at the top, then press Enter or the ✓ Submit button to lock it in. Fill every slot on the board and the level explodes with confetti before the next one slides into view.

Made a mistake? Click a lit-up tile in the ring or press Backspace to peel the last letter off, or tap an earlier tile to jump back to that point in your word. Found every word that can use that letter combination already? A word you have already discovered gives a gentle shake so you know to try something new. There are forty hand-designed levels, starting with friendly four-letter sets and building up to six-letter boards packed with tricky longer words. Your highest level reached is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back to continue the climb.

Controls

  • Click or tap ring tiles to spell a word — the physical keyboard letters pick tiles too
  • Backspace or clicking a placed tile erases letters, Enter or ✓ submits
  • Esc or P pauses (P only when no ring tile of that letter is left), Space starts or resumes, and the Reset button restarts at any time
  • 💡 Hint reveals one free letter of one remaining word — it costs 100 points

Scoring and tips

  • New words score 30 points for three letters, 60 for four, 100 for five and 160 for six
  • Clearing a whole board pays a bonus of 200 points plus 50 for every level behind you
  • Start with the longest slot on the board — the short words usually hide inside it
  • Look for plurals: if you find "star", "stars" is almost always waiting too
  • Reveal letters with the hint button only when you are truly stuck — it eats your score
