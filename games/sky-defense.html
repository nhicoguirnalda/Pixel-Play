Sky Defense

How to Play Sky Defense

Sky Defense puts you behind the crosshair of a friendly sky city's defence tower. Colourful little aircraft and drones buzz in from the left and head straight for the rooftops, and only you can stop them. Slide the mouse or your finger to move the crosshair, then click or tap to fire. Every shot that lands pops the aircraft into a burst of sparks and adds points to your score.

Your tower carries a magazine of 6 shots and reloads automatically when the clip runs dry, so spray-and-pray has a price. Hits are what matter: each one builds a combo, and consecutive hits multiply your score up to 5×. A miss costs no points, but it does break the streak. The city starts with 5 health — every aircraft that slips off the right-hand side of the sky takes one point of it away. When the health reaches zero the round ends, so guard the rooftops and go for the high-value streaks.

Controls

  • Move the mouse or drag your finger to aim the crosshair
  • Click or tap the sky to fire one round
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume
  • P or Escape to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: tap the sky to shoot and use the Reload and Pause buttons

Tips for a high score

  • Let an aircraft drift into the middle of the sky before you shoot — a miss near the edge is a wasted round.
  • Protect a long combo. Three hits in a row is worth more than three rushed, scattered shots.
  • Keep an eye on the magazine bar. Fire your last round early, then reload while the sky is quiet.
  • Small drones are worth the most and they are the fastest — lead your aim slightly ahead of them.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
