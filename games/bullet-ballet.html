How to Play Bullet Ballet

Bullet Ballet is an endless single-arena boss rush built around three lives. Your orb drifts around a 600×600 dance floor while a luminous boss spins out walls of light. You never press a fire button: your orb auto-fires at the boss, so your attention goes into reading patterns and slipping through the gaps.

Three bosses cycle in sequence, each with its own silhouette, colour and bullet vocabulary. The Petal Weaver opens with rings and spirals; the Prism Warden adds gated rings and four-spoke crossfire; the Ember Crown mixes every pattern into five phases. Each phase carries 500, 560 or 640 HP, and every completed cycle multiplies that pool by 45%, with a small extra step per archetype. Below 30% health a boss enters rage, blending two patterns at a faster tempo.

You start every run with 3 lives. A clean hit costs 1 life and grants a brief invulnerability window plus knockback; a shield charge absorbs the hit instead. Boss damage still grows each cycle, and the HUD shows that threat value, but every unshielded hit is always one life. Clearing a phase can drop a power-up — a +1 LIFE heart, shield, extra stream, slow-motion or damage boost. Beating a boss lets you pick one of three upgrades with 1, 2 or 3 or by clicking: +3 shot damage, a faster fire interval, an extra stream, shield charges or a +1 life Field Repair. Finishing a full boss cycle also restores 1 life. Upgrades stop after the fifth boss.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to move your orb
  • 1, 2 and 3 to pick an upgrade card, or click a card
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume, P / Esc to pause, the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone, use the on-screen direction pad below the arena

Tips for a longer run

  • Watch the boss, not your orb — bullets telegraph a beat before they launch.
  • Ring patterns leave a safe pocket between the spokes; find it early and drift into it.
  • Shields are precious: each one cancels a hit that would otherwise cost a whole life.
  • Focused Beam pays off against deep phase bars, while Split Shot and Quick Trigger stack well early.
