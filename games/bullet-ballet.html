How to Play Bullet Ballet

Bullet Ballet is an endless single-arena boss rush. Your orb drifts around a 600×600 dance floor while a luminous boss spins out walls of light. You never press a fire button: your orb auto-fires at the boss on its own, so all of your attention goes into reading patterns and slipping through the gaps.

Three bosses now cycle in sequence, each with its own silhouette, colour and bullet vocabulary. The Petal Weaver opens with rings and spirals; the Prism Warden adds gated rings and a four-spoke crossfire; the Ember Crown mixes every pattern into five phases. Every boss keeps a health bar and phase changes, and below 30% health it enters a rage state that blends two patterns at a faster tempo. Defeating one boss does not reset the arena: the next archetype arrives tougher than the last, and once all three have fallen the cycle repeats with stronger bosses.

Clearing a phase drops a power-up — heal, shield, extra shot, slow-motion or a damage boost. Drift into the icon to collect it; they slide towards you when close. Beating a whole boss lets you choose one of three upgrades with 1, 2 or 3, or by clicking a card: more damage, a faster fire interval, an extra auto-fire stream, shield charges or a 45 HP repair. You start with 100 HP; each hit costs 10 HP and knocks you back, followed by one second of invulnerability.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to move your orb
  • 1, 2 and 3 to pick an upgrade card, or click the card with the mouse or a finger
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume, P / Esc to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone, use the on-screen direction pad below the arena

Tips for a longer run

  • Watch the boss, not your orb — bullets telegraph a beat before they launch.
  • Ring patterns leave a safe pocket between the spokes; find it early and drift into it.
  • Grab shields before a boss phase ends; they absorb a full hit and protect your HP.
  • Split Shot and Quick Trigger stack well early, while Focused Beam pays off against the Ember Crown's deep health bar.
