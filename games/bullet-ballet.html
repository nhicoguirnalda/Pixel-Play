How to Play Bullet Ballet

Bullet Ballet is a single-arena boss fight. Your glowing orb drifts around a 600×600 dance floor while a huge luminous boss sits in the centre, spinning out dense walls of light. You never press a fire button: your orb auto-fires at the boss on its own, so all of your attention goes into reading the patterns and slipping through the gaps.

The boss cycles through four phases. Each phase switches the pattern — expanding rings, a rotating spiral, and spread shots aimed straight at you — and every phase pushes out more bullets than the last. You start with 100 HP; each bullet that clips you costs 10 HP and shoves you backwards, with a short moment of invulnerability to help you recover. Drain the boss's health bar to move on, and clear the final phase to win the run and collect a big score bonus. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to move your orb
  • Space or Enter to start, pause and resume
  • P or Esc to pause, R to restart the fight
  • On a phone, use the on-screen direction pad below the arena

Tips for surviving every phase

  • Watch the boss, not your orb — bullets telegraph a beat before they launch.
  • Ring patterns leave a safe pocket between the spokes; find it early and drift into it.
  • Spirals are beaten by circling in the same direction, just slower than the rotation.
  • Keep moving when you take a hit. The knockback is a gift: it puts distance between you and the next volley.
  • Save a little reaction space near the edges. Cornering yourself against a ring is how runs end.
