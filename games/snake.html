How to Play Neon Snake

You control a glowing snake moving across a 20×20 grid. Each orb you eat makes the snake longer and scores 10 points — and the game gets a little faster. The run ends when you hit a wall or your own body, so think two turns ahead before you commit.

The board is a neon arena lit by drifting glow pulses: green orbs pulse under your head, the tail leaves a fading trail behind it, and the wall frames hum brighter as your score climbs. Because the grid holds a fixed number of cells, every orb you eat also shrinks the safe space you have left — a run that feels easy at 50 points starts to feel crowded at 250 and genuinely tight past 400. That slow squeeze is the whole game: you are not racing a timer, you are racing the length of your own body.

Speed steps up every 50 points, and each step makes the gap between a good decision and a fatal one that much smaller. Neon Snake is a pure, one-thumb take on the arcade classic — no power-ups to learn, no menus to click through, just you, a growing tail and a grid that rewards patience over panic. Chase a personal best, then try to beat it with a deliberately tighter spiral or a more aggressive route down the middle.

Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD to steer
  • Space to start or pause, P to pause, R to restart
  • On a phone: swipe across the game board or use the on-screen direction pad

Tips for a high score

  • Keep to the outside of the grid — the middle fills up fast once you pass 150 points.
  • Never double back. A quick 180° turn into your own neck is the classic way to lose.
  • Speed rises every 50 points. Short, controlled moves beat panic near the walls.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.
