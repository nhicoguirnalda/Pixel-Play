How to Play Scope Elite

Scope Elite puts you behind the glass on a watchtower overlooking a desert testing valley. Robot scouts, darting runners and slow heavy walkers cross the range at four different distances, and your job is simple: knock out the mission quota before the clock runs out. Clear all three missions back to back and the valley is secure. Every bot that slips off the far edge of the scene costs your tower one point of integrity — lose all three and the run ends right there.

Each mission lasts about sixty seconds and demands a bigger quota than the one before. Bots further away fill fewer pixels, but distant hits pay more points, and heavy walkers are worth chasing down before they reach the rock cover. Later missions mix in more darting bots that dash between boulders and crouch out of sight, so pick your moments.

Controls

  • Click the view once to lock the mouse and sweep the valley — or move it freely to place the crosshair
  • Hold Shift or the right mouse button to look through the scope
  • Left click or F to fire a shot
  • Space hops the tower for a fresh angle — staying airborne costs accuracy (Space also starts a new run)
  • Enter, P or Esc pauses; R restarts
  • On a phone: drag to aim, tap to fire, tap the 🔍 button to toggle the scope

Scope, breath and bullet drop

The scope magnifies the valley and shrinks your shot spread to almost nothing, but it sways — and the longer you hold your breath, the more it sways. A steady-breath meter drains while you are scoped and refills the moment you release, so short, calm peeks beat long ones. Firing from the hip is fast but sloppy, and distant bots need you to hold slightly above them to compensate for bullet drop.

Scoring and strategy

  • Every hit pays points scaled by distance — the further the bot, the better
  • Clip the antenna sensor on top of a bot for double points on that hit
  • Land consecutive hits to build a streak multiplier of up to 4×
  • Finishing a mission banks a clear bonus and repairs one integrity point
  • Tip: let darters settle behind a rock before you squeeze the trigger
