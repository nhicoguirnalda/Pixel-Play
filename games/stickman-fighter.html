How to Play Stickman Fighter

Stickman Fighter drops you into a neon side-view arena where waves of rival stick fighters march in from both edges of the screen. You only need three moves — a fast punch, a heavier kick and a low guard — but chaining them clears a wave. Every rival walks up, winds up with a clear flashing tell, then throws a swing: step away as it lands and you collect a dodge bonus, or hold the low guard and soak most of the damage.

Land a clean hit and your combo counter climbs, adding a bigger bonus with every uninterrupted blow. Knock a rival out and they tumble off with a cartoon ring of stars above their head. Each wave brings more fighters with quicker feet and tougher heads, so never get cornered: keep moving, pick rivals off at the edges, and use the two-second breather between waves to recover — clearing a wave heals a little and pays a bonus. The fight is endless, and your best score is saved in this browser.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to walk around the arena
  • ↓ or S to crouch and guard — you still slide slowly while guarding
  • Z or click for a quick short punch, X or Shift for a slower kick with knockback
  • Space also punches during a fight, P, Esc or Enter pause, R restarts
  • On a phone: use the ◀ / 🛡 Guard / 👊 Punch / 🦵 Kick / ▶ buttons under the arena

Tips for lasting longer

  • Watch the flash, not the fighter: every rival telegraphs, so a short step back turns the swing into a dodge bonus.
  • Kicks push crowds away — one kick creates the breathing room you need.
  • Guard only once the swing has started — holding guard too early stops your movement and lets rivals surround you.
  • Combos reset the moment you get hit — slip away and re-engage instead of trading blows.
  • Your best score is saved automatically in this browser.

Scoring

Punches and kicks pay points per hit, scaled up by your live combo counter. Clean dodges add a bonus, every knockout pays extra, and clearing a wave awards a wave bonus plus a health top-up. The HUD tracks Score, Best, Wave and HP at a glance.
