How to Play Stickman Fighter

Stickman Fighter drops you into a neon side-view arena where rival fighters march in from both edges. You only need three moves — a fast punch, a heavier kick and a low guard — but chaining them clears a wave. Every rival walks up, flashes a clear wind-up, then swings: step away as it lands for a dodge bonus, or hold guard and soak most of the damage.

Every fourth wave is a boss fight, starting at wave 4. A giant named stick fighter enters under a flashing WARNING banner with its own health bar, then attacks with a heavy charge, a ground slam and a three-hit flurry. At 50% HP the boss enters phase 2, hits harder, moves faster and calls in one or two regular fighters. You can block a boss hit, sidestep the charge or run out of the slam ring; the wave will not clear until the boss and its reinforcements are down. Boss health, damage and speed climb with every boss, and killing one pays a large score bonus before wave clear.

Waves mix three silhouettes. Fast green Strikers close distance and swing quickly, while hulking orange Brutes soak damage and hit much harder. Every wave raises enemy HP, damage, speed and spawn count and shortens attack cooldowns. Beaten rivals drop glowing pickups — heal, damage, speed, shield and combo boosts — and clearing every third wave offers three permanent upgrades.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to walk around the arena
  • ↓ or S to crouch and guard — you still slide slowly while guarding
  • Z or click for a quick punch, X or Shift for a slower kick with knockback
  • Every third wave, press 1, 2 or 3 (or click a card) to pick an upgrade
  • Space punches, P / Esc / Enter pause, the Reset button restarts
  • On a phone: use the ◀ / 🛡 Guard / 👊 Punch / 🦵 Kick / ▶ buttons under the arena

Tips for lasting longer

  • Watch the flash, not the fighter: a short step back turns every telegraphed swing into a dodge bonus.
  • Save a kick for a Brute — the knockback buys time to deal with faster Strikers.
  • Hold guard through a boss flurry, then punish the recovery with kicks.
