How to Play Stickman Fighter

Stickman Fighter drops you into a neon side-view arena where waves of rival fighters march in from both edges. You only need three moves — a fast punch, a heavier kick and a low guard — but chaining them clears a wave. Every rival walks up, flashes a clear wind-up, then swings: step away as it lands to collect a dodge bonus, or hold guard and soak most of the damage.

Every connecting punch and kick now throws sparks, an expanding shockwave ring, a floating damage number and a brief hit-stop with a little screen shake. Hits landed from the left side — you standing left of the target and swinging right — burst into a bright orange-and-white cone with a stronger double ring, so a left-side blow always reads differently from a return strike. Knockouts still send rivals tumbling with cartoon stars.

Beaten rivals sometimes drop glowing pickups: a green cross heals, a red arrow boosts damage for 12 seconds, cyan chevrons boost speed, a violet bubble adds a shield charge that absorbs one clean hit, and a gold star extends your combo window. Clearing every third wave freezes the arena and offers three permanent run upgrades — damage, speed, heal, guard strength or combo duration. Guard upgrades make blocks sting less, and combo upgrades keep your multiplier alive longer.

Controls

  • ← → or AD to walk around the arena
  • ↓ or S to crouch and guard — you still slide slowly while guarding
  • Z or click for a quick punch, X or Shift for a slower kick with knockback
  • After waves 3, 6, 9 and so on, press 1, 2 or 3 (or click a card) to pick an upgrade
  • Space punches, P / Esc / Enter pause, R restarts
  • On a phone: use the ◀ / 🛡 Guard / 👊 Punch / 🦵 Kick / ▶ buttons under the arena

Tips for lasting longer

  • Watch the flash, not the fighter: a short step back turns every telegraphed swing into a dodge bonus.
  • Kicks push crowds away — one kick creates the breathing room you need.
  • Grab shield and heal pickups before chasing a knockout; they are worth more than the extra points.
